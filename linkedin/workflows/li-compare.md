# WORKFLOW: /li-compare (Model & Tool Comparison)

**Trigger Command:** `/li-compare <Model A vs Model B, or Tool A vs Tool B>`  
**Goal:** Produce a rigorous, side-by-side evidence-based comparison.

---

## EXECUTION STEPS

1. **Primary Benchmark Search:** Gather independent benchmarks (LMSYS Arena, SWE-bench, GAIA, Artificial Analysis) for both targets.
2. **Pricing & Latency:** Compare input/output token pricing per 1M tokens, speed (tokens/sec), and time-to-first-token.
3. **Vendor vs Independent:** Explicitly label vendor-reported numbers vs independent community benchmarks.
4. **"Best at What" Categorization:** Define specific use cases (e.g., "Model A leads for agentic coding, Model B leads for low-cost RAG retrieval").
5. **Output Deliverable:** Comparison matrix and ready-to-post LinkedIn breakdown.
